PlantHappy Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

PlantHappy is a plant care app that helps you track watering, fertilizing, notes, and photos for your plants. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

The short version: PlantHappy has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no servers of its own. Your plants, notes, photos, and profile details stay on your device. The only information that may leave your device is location data, which is optional and entirely under your control. It is sent to weather and place-lookup services only so the app can show local weather.

Information stored on your device

The following is saved only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Plants you add, including names, types, locations within your home, birthdays, and care schedules
  • Activity history, including watering and fertilizing dates and notes
  • Photos you choose to attach to a plant
  • Your first name, last name, and email address, if you enter them in Settings
  • Your saved weather location and reminder settings

We (the developer) cannot see or access any of this information.

Location and weather (optional)

PlantHappy shows current weather on its home screen. This is the only feature that sends any information off your device, and it is optional. Location data, including your precise device location, is collected only if you choose to allow it, and you can change that choice at any time.

The app can get weather in one of two ways, and you decide which, if either, is used:

  • Device location (precise): only if you grant PlantHappy location permission and have not saved a weather location in Settings. The app reads your device's precise location, then sends coordinates rounded to about 1 km to Open-Meteo to get the current weather. To show your city name, it sends the same rounded coordinates to your device's built-in geocoding service (on Android, provided by Google) and, if that is unavailable, to Nominatim, a service run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation. If the weather lookup fails, the app may send your precise coordinates to your device's built-in geocoding service to show your city name instead.
  • A location you search for (approximate): if you search for a place in Settings, the text you type is sent to Nominatim to find matching places. The saved place's coordinates, rounded to about 1 km, are then used for weather and city-name lookups as described above. Your device's own location is not used.

You control this collection:

  • Choose Don't allow when PlantHappy asks for location permission, or turn the permission off at any time in your device's settings.
  • Save a weather location in Settings to use it instead of your device's location.
  • If you deny location permission and do not save a weather location, no location data leaves your device. The rest of the app works normally; only the weather display is unavailable.

Location data is used only to show weather. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose, and we (the developer) never receive it.

These services receive each request along with your device's IP address, as with any internet request. Their handling of that data is governed by their own policies: Open-Meteo terms and privacy, the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy, and, for your device's built-in geocoding service on Android, Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on watering or fertilizing reminders, PlantHappy schedules notifications locally on your device. No reminder information is sent anywhere.

Backups

You can create a backup file that contains your plants, activity history, photos, plant locations, and profile details. The file is saved wherever you choose, such as a folder on your device or your own cloud storage. PlantHappy does not upload or host backups. Backup files are not encrypted, so store them somewhere private.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information or share it with anyone other than the weather and geocoding services described above, and only when you allow location use.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.
  • We do not require an account or sign-in.

Google Play

If you install PlantHappy from Google Play, Google may collect information about your device and app usage under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control that collection.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual plants and activity entries in the app. Uninstalling PlantHappy removes all of its data from your device. Any backup files you created remain wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Children

PlantHappy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
