PlantHappy Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

PlantHappy is a plant care app that helps you track watering, fertilizing, notes, and photos for your plants. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

The short version: PlantHappy has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no servers of its own. Your plants, notes, photos, and profile details stay on your device. The only information that leaves your device is location data sent to weather and place-lookup services so the app can show local weather.

Information stored on your device

The following is saved only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Plants you add, including names, types, locations within your home, birthdays, and care schedules
  • Activity history, including watering and fertilizing dates and notes
  • Photos you choose to attach to a plant
  • Your first name, last name, and email address, if you enter them in Settings
  • Your saved weather location and reminder settings

We (the developer) cannot see or access any of this information.

Location and weather

PlantHappy shows current weather on its home screen. To do this, it uses either a location you search for in Settings or, if you allow it, your device's location.

  • Weather: the app sends approximate coordinates (rounded to about 1 km) to Open-Meteo to get the current weather.
  • Place names: to show your city name, the app sends your device's coordinates to Nominatim, a service run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation. When you search for a weather location in Settings, the text you type is sent to Nominatim to find matching places.

These services receive the request along with your device's IP address, as with any internet request. Their handling of that data is governed by their own policies: Open-Meteo terms and privacy and the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Location permission is optional. If you deny it, you can still pick a weather location by searching, or use the app without weather.

Notifications

If you turn on watering or fertilizing reminders, PlantHappy schedules notifications locally on your device. No reminder information is sent anywhere.

Backups

You can create a backup file that contains your plants, activity history, photos, plant locations, and profile details. The file is saved wherever you choose, such as a folder on your device or your own cloud storage. PlantHappy does not upload or host backups. Backup files are not encrypted, so store them somewhere private.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.
  • We do not require an account or sign-in.

Google Play

If you install PlantHappy from Google Play, Google may collect information about your device and app usage under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control that collection.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual plants and activity entries in the app. Uninstalling PlantHappy removes all of its data from your device. Any backup files you created remain wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Children

PlantHappy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
