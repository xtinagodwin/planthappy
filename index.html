PlantHappy

Track watering, fertilizing and plant progress with reminders and photo logs.

Now Available on the App Store and Google Play

PlantHappy helps you keep your houseplants healthy by remembering when each one needs water and fertilizer, so you don't have to.

Add your plants, set how often each one needs care, and PlantHappy shows you exactly which plants need attention today. Mark tasks done with a tap, snooze them until tomorrow, or move them to another day.

Your daily care dashboard

  • See today's watering and fertilizing tasks at a glance
  • Filter tasks by type or by where your plants live, like "Kitchen" or "Bedroom window"
  • Mark one plant or all due plants as done at once
  • Snooze a task until tomorrow or reschedule it for a later date
  • Check the current local weather right on the home screen

Care reminders

  • Get watering and fertilizing reminders at the time of day you choose
  • Reminders are scheduled for each plant's due date
  • Turn watering and fertilizing reminders on or off separately

Plant care history

  • Keep a dated log of every watering, fertilizing, and note
  • Add notes about new leaves, repotting, pests, or anything else
  • Attach photos to track how each plant changes over time

Progress photos

  • Browse each plant's photos in its own gallery, newest first
  • Compare two photos side by side to see how much your plant has grown

Organize your collection

  • Save each plant's name, type, location, and birthday
  • Group plants by location and filter your tasks by room
  • Edit or remove plants whenever your collection changes

Back up and restore

  • Save a backup file with your plants, care history, and photos
  • Store it wherever you like, such as your device or your own cloud storage
  • Restore it on a new phone to pick up right where you left off

Private by design

  • No account or sign-in required
  • No ads and no tracking
  • Your plants, notes, and photos stay on your device
  • Location is optional and only used to show local weather

Whether you have one pothos on the windowsill or a whole jungle in the living room, PlantHappy makes it easy to give every plant the care it needs.
